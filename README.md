## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **Manager Sales Performance & Comparison Dashboard** designed to evaluate team performance, track revenue targets, and analyze sales dynamics across individual managers and field couriers.

The dashboard allows team leads and executives to perform comparative timeframe analysis using key metrics such as **Month-over-Month (MoM)**, **Same Period Last Year (SPLY)**, and **Year-over-Year (YoY)** growth.

---

## Key Metrics & Analytical Features
* **Manager & Courier Leaderboard:** Ranks sales managers and couriers based on revenue generated and growth percentages to identify top performers.
* **Comparative Sales Metrics:** Tracks `Total Sales`, `Sales Last Month (LM)`, `Sales SPLY`, and `Sales YoY` growth.
* **Regional & Temporal Breakdown:** Visualizes sales trends by region and compares current performance against previous monthly and yearly periods.
* **Interactive Filtering:** Enables dynamic slicing by **Year**, **Month**, **Manager**, and **Courier**.

---

## Tools & Technologies
* **Analytics Platform:** Power BI / Microsoft Excel
* **Data Modeling:** Custom DAX Measures, Time Intelligence (YoY, MoM, SPLY)
* **Visuals:** Matrix Visuals, Combination Bar/Line Charts, KPI Cards, Dynamic Slicers
